# Scope Definition — ShiftSwap MVP

## In Scope (Sprint 1 MVP)
- Employees can post open shifts they need covered.
- Qualified coworkers can view and claim open shifts.
- Managers can approve or deny claimed shifts.
- System sends basic notifications for key events (new open shift, claim, approval/denial).
- Audit trail captures who posted, claimed, and approved/denied, with timestamps.
- Managers can view current and recent swap activity in one place.

## Out of Scope (Sprint 1)
- Payroll and timekeeping integrations.
- Slack integration.
- Advanced overtime automation (beyond manager-facing flags).
- Enterprise SSO or broader identity-platform redesign.
